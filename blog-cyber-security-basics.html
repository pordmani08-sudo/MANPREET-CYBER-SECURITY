CYBERSECURITY BASICS · BEGINNER GUIDE

What Is Cyber Security?

Cyber security means protecting computers, phones, networks, accounts and data from unauthorized access, damage, scams and other digital threats. It combines safe habits, technology and awareness.

Why does cyber security matter?

We use the internet for communication, study, shopping, banking and work. If an account or device is not protected, personal information can be exposed, money can be stolen, or important files can be lost. Good security habits reduce these risks.

Common online threats

  • Phishing: Fake emails, texts or websites that try to trick people into sharing passwords or financial details.
  • Malware: Harmful software that can disrupt a device, spy on activity or damage files.
  • Password attacks: Attempts to guess, reuse or steal account passwords.
  • Social engineering: Tricks that pressure people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

7 simple ways to stay safer online

  1. Use a long, unique password for every important account.
  2. Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever it is available.
  3. Never share one-time passwords or login codes with anyone.
  4. Check website addresses and sender details before clicking links.
  5. Install updates for your phone, computer, browser and apps.
  6. Back up important files to a separate, trusted location.
  7. Download apps and files only from sources you trust.

What is ethical hacking?

Ethical hacking is authorized security testing. A tester works within clearly agreed permission and scope to identify weaknesses so they can be fixed. Never test a website, account, network or device unless you own it or have explicit permission.

Start learning

Build your knowledge one topic at a time: account protection, phishing awareness, safe browsing, backups and basic network security. Practice only in legal training labs or systems where you have permission.

Remember: Cyber security is not a one-time task. Small, consistent habits help protect your digital life.
